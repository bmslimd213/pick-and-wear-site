Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy describes how Pick & Wear uses data in connection with its internal publishing tools and public content.

Our publishing tools

Pick & Wear uses internal tools to prepare, approve, schedule and publish content to social platforms. These tools are intended for use by Pick & Wear and its authorized operator, not by the general public.

Pinterest data

When connected to Pinterest through the Pinterest API, our internal publishing tool may access information associated with the authenticated Pick & Wear account that is necessary to manage our own boards and Pins, create approved Pins, and confirm publishing results. We do not use the tool to collect personal information about people who view or interact with our Pins.

Instagram and other platforms

Pick & Wear may use similar internal publishing workflows for its own accounts on other social platforms. Access is limited to information required to manage and publish content for those accounts.

Visitors and personal information

This website does not require registration and does not intentionally collect names, email addresses, payment information, or other personal information from visitors. We do not sell personal information.

Third-party and affiliate links

Our content may link to third-party retailers, affiliate partners, or other external websites. Once you follow an external link, the destination service operates under its own terms and privacy practices. Pick & Wear does not control how those third parties process information.

Data sharing and retention

Data accessed through platform APIs is used only as needed to operate the relevant Pick & Wear publishing workflow. We do not sell platform data. Access can be revoked through the settings of the connected platform.

Changes

We may update this policy when our publishing tools or practices change. The current version will remain available at this URL.

Contact

Questions about this Privacy Policy can be directed to Pick & Wear through its official social profile. A dedicated contact address may be added as the project expands.
